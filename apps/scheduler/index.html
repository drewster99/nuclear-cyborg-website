Drew's Video + Socials Scheduler

A local-first publishing workstation for YouTube™ creators. AI-generated descriptions, multi-platform social scheduling, and comment moderation — all running on your Mac, with your credentials stored in the system Keychain.

See features

Built for the way creators actually work

Drop a video in. Get a draft description from the transcript. Pick the social posts you want, edit each one, schedule the lot. Move on.

📝

AI descriptions & tags

Claude turns the video's transcript into an SEO-shaped description and tag list. The prompts are editable templates, not hardcoded.

📡

Multi-platform social posts

Generate per-platform copy from a single template, edit each card individually, then post now or schedule. Built-in OAuth for every supported network.

⏰

Scheduled publishing

Set a publish time on the video, and every approved social post fires at that exact moment. Reschedule freely — concurrent edits are locked safely.

🎙️

Multiple transcript backends

YouTube™ auto-captions, MLX Whisper, whisper.cpp, and macOS Speech Recognition — pick the active transcript per video, or let auto-actions handle it.

🗂️

Multiple channels, one workspace

Each project pairs a YouTube™ video platform channel with its own social accounts, templates, blocklist, and auto-action defaults. Switch between them without re-authing.

🛡️

Comment moderation

Per-project keyword and regex blocklist that runs on a configurable interval — or fire it on demand and see the actual hits.

⚡

Tier-aware templates

Videos auto-classify by length — hook, short, segment, full video — and templates declare which tiers they apply to. The right copy for the right format, every time.

📊

Per-video activity log

Created, uploaded, metadata-updated (with per-field diff), scheduled, published, social-post-sent — every event captured in a sidebar feed you can audit.

🍎

Native macOS app

A self-contained Mac app that runs quietly in the background and stays out of your way. Open it when you need it; your schedule keeps running when you don't.

From upload to live in five steps

Each step is automatable; flip the auto-action toggles and the next stage starts on its own when the previous one finishes.

01

Upload or import

Drop a new file, or pull an existing video back from YouTube™ with its description, tags, and transcript intact.

02

Transcribe

Auto-captions or on-device Whisper — pick a backend, or let the project's default decide.

03

Generate

Description, tags, and per-platform social copy come out of editable Claude prompts.

04

Review & schedule

Edit each social card individually. Approve them. Pick a publish time.

05

Publish

The scheduler flips the video public and fires every approved post in the same moment.

Local-first. Always.

Your videos, transcripts, drafts, and OAuth tokens never leave your machine. Credentials live in the macOS Keychain. The only network calls go to the platforms you've explicitly connected and to Claude for the AI generation steps you trigger.

Getting an Anthropic (Claude) API key

The Scheduler talks to Claude directly with your own key. Usage is billed to you by Anthropic (Claude) at their published rates, and nothing passes through us.

  1. Create a Claude Console account at platform.claude.com. Sign in if you already have one.
  2. Add a payment method under Billing. The API is pay as you go, so a key without billing won't work.
  3. Create a key on the API keys page. Copy it right away; the full key is shown only once.
  4. Paste it into the Scheduler's settings. It's stored in your macOS Keychain, never in a file.

More detail in the Anthropic (Claude) getting started guide.
