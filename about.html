About Nuclear Cyborg Corp

An AI and software consulting company and App Store publisher, founded by Andrew Benson

Andrew Benson, Founder

Andrew Benson founded Nuclear Cyborg Corp. He builds iOS and macOS apps and works on AI and machine learning, and he is based in Minnesota.

Nuclear Cyborg Corp has published apps on Apple's App Store since 2015. Today it also offers AI development, enterprise AI consulting and software architecture services.

Open Source

Andrew publishes more than 100 public repositories on GitHub, including Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and Swift packages:

  • AIBattleground: send one prompt to 40+ large language models and compare their responses
  • drews-xcode-mcp: an MCP server for working with Xcode
  • appstore-mcp-server: App Store search and lookup
  • InternetNamesMCP: domain name and social handle availability
