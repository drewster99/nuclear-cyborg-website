Collection and Use of Personal Information

Personal information is data that can be used to identify or contact a single person.

You may be asked to provide your personal information anytime you are in contact with Nuclear Cyborg Corp or a Nuclear Cyborg Corp affiliated company.  Nuclear Cyborg Corp and its affiliates may share this personal information with each other and use it consistent with this Privacy Policy.  They may also combine it with other information to provide and improve our products, services, content, and advertising.  You are not required to provide the personal information that we have requested, but, if you chose not to do so, in many cases we will not be able to provide you with our products or services or respond to any queries you may have.

Here are some examples of the types of personal information Nuclear Cyborg Corp may collect and how we may use it:

·      When you create a Nuclear Cyborg Corp account, purchase a product, download a software update, contact us, or participate in an online survey, chat room, or forum, we may collect a variety of information, including your name, mailing address, phone number, email address, contact preferences, age and/or date of birth, gender, social media account identifiers, and credit card / payment information.

·      When you upload or post photos, videos, or audio recordings to our apps, websites, forums, social media, or chat groups.

·      When you share your content with family and friends using Nuclear Cyborg Corp products, send gift certificates and products, or invite others to participate in Nuclear Cyborg Corp services or forums, Nuclear Cyborg Corp may collect the information you provide about those people such as name, mailing address, email address, and phone number.  Nuclear Cyborg Corp will use such information to fulfill your requests, provide the relevant product or service, or for anti-fraud purposes.

·      When you purchase or lease our apps, in-app purchases, subscriptions, services, upgrades, or products, we collect information about the transaction, such as identifiers, date/time, prices, and currency.  For products purchased outside of the Apple App Store or Google Play, we may collect your name, phone number, email and/or account identifier, payment details, billing address, and shipping, delivery, and fulfillment details.

·      The personal information we collect allows us to keep you posted on Nuclear Cyborg Corp’s latest product announcements, software updates, and upcoming events.  If you don’t want to be on our mailing list, you can opt out anytime by contacting us.

·      If you enter a sweepstake, contest, or similar promotion we may use the information you provide to administer those programs.

We retain personal information for as long as needed to deliver or operate the app, software, or service.  For example, if you have a Nuclear Cyborg Corp account, all personal information associated with your account will be retained for as long as you have the account, plus additional time to allow for handling of customer service issues.

If your account has been sanctioned for violations of our Terms of Use or User Agreement, information about the sanction will be retained indefinitely, even if you delete your account.

We may retain personal information to resolve disputes, enforce our Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, or any applicable app-specific or service-specific User Agreement, or to comply with a legal requirement.

Cookies and Other Technologies

Nuclear Cyborg Corp’s websites, online services, interactive applications, email messages, and advertisements may use cookies and other similar technologies, such as pixel tags and web beacons. These technologies help us better understand user behavior, tell us which parts of our websites people have visited, and facilitate and measure the effectiveness of advertisements and web searches.  We treat information collected by cookies and other technologies as non‑personal information.  However, to the extent that Internet Protocol (IP) addresses or similar identifiers are considered personal information by local law, we also treat these identifiers as personal information. Similarly, to the extent that non-personal information is combined with personal information, we treat the combined information as personal information for the purposes of this Privacy Policy.

On iOS devices, we may request that you consent to using the devices Advertising Identifier.

You can choose to deny this consent by enabling Limit Ad Tracking (prior to iOS 14.5) or Ask App Not to Track (App Tracking Transparency, iOS 14.5 and later), or responding appropriately if you are prompted by the app.

If you enable this feature on your mobile device, third-party apps, such as those provided by Nuclear Cyborg Corp, cannot use the device’s Advertising Identifier to serve you targeted ads.  You will still see the same number of ads, but the ads may be less relevant to you.

If you consent to this tracking and later wish to withdraw consent, you may do so in the device’s Settings at any time.

Nuclear Cyborg Corp and our partners also use cookies and other technologies to remember personal information when you use our website, online services, and applications.  Our goal in these cases is to make your experience with Nuclear Cyborg Corp more convenient and personal.  For example, knowing your first name lets us welcome you the next time you visit the Nuclear Cyborg Corp sites and stores.  Knowing your country and language − and if you are an educator, your school − helps us provide a customized and more useful shopping experience.  Knowing someone using your computer or device has shopped for a certain product or used a particular service helps us make our advertising and email communications more relevant to your interests.  And knowing your contact information, and information about your computer or device helps us personalize your app experience, and provide you with better customer service.

Check with your browser provider to find out how to disable cookies. Please note that certain features of Nuclear Cyborg Corp websites will not be available once cookies are disabled.

As is true of most internet services, we gather some information automatically and store it in log files. This information includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type and language, Internet service provider (ISP), referring and exit websites and applications, operating system, date/time stamp, and clickstream data.

We use this information to understand and analyze trends, to administer the site, to learn about user behavior on the site, to improve our product and services, and to gather demographic information about our user base as a whole.  Nuclear Cyborg Corp may use this information in our marketing and advertising services.

In some of our email messages, we use a “click-through URL” linked to content on the Nuclear Cyborg Corp website.  When customers click one of these URLs, they pass through a separate web server before arriving at the destination page on our website.  This separate server may be owned and operated by Nuclear Cyborg Corp or may be owned and operated by a third-party data processor.  We track this click-through data to help us determine interest in particular topics and measure the effectiveness of our customer communications. If you prefer not to be tracked in this way, you should not click text or graphic links in the email messages.

Pixel tags enable us to send email messages in a format customers can read, and they tell us whether mail has been opened.  We may use this information to reduce or eliminate messages sent to customers.

Biometrics and Face Data

No Nuclear Cyborg Corp apps collect, store, transmit, or share any biometric or face data.

The app “Santa Face Live Moji Mask” uses live face data to animate a 3D cartoon face and head model with the same movements and expressions as the user’s face.  This data is processed ephemerally, on-device, while the app is running, and is then immediately discarded.  Additionally, neither the animated cartoon expressions nor the videos created by the user are transmitted off-device, except as directed by the user’s sharing actions within the app.

Drew’s Video Socials Scheduler

Drew’s Video Socials Scheduler is a macOS application that runs on your own computer. Your videos, transcripts, drafts, generated text, project settings, templates, and activity history are stored locally on your Mac. Nuclear Cyborg Corp operates no server for this app, receives no copy of that material, and has no access to it.

To publish on your behalf, the app asks you to sign in to the video and social media accounts you choose to connect. The resulting access credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain on your computer. They are never transmitted to Nuclear Cyborg Corp, and we do not receive, store, or have access to your connected accounts or their contents.

Information the app retrieves from a connected account — such as your account name, the videos and posts you manage, and comments on them — stays on your Mac and is used only to provide the features you invoke. It is never used for advertising, ad targeting, ad measurement, or attribution, and it is never used to build profiles of you or anyone else. It is never sold, licensed, rented, or shared with advertising networks, data brokers, or any other third party. The analytics and advertising data processors listed elsewhere in this Privacy Policy are not used by this app.

Content is published only when you ask for it. The app prepares drafts for your review, and material is sent to a connected account only after you have approved it and set the time at which it should go out.

Text generation features send the material you select — such as a video transcript, a title, or your own prompt template — to Anthropic (Claude) using an API key that you supply and that Anthropic bills to you. Those requests go directly from your Mac to Anthropic and do not pass through Nuclear Cyborg Corp. Credentials for your connected accounts are never included in them.

You can disconnect any connected account at any time from within the app. Disconnecting deletes the stored credentials and any cached account data from your Mac and, where the provider supports it, revokes the app’s access at the provider. Removing the application and its data folder deletes everything the app has stored. Because we hold no copy of this data, no request to us is required, and the identity verification described under “Personal Data Deletion & Privacy Questions” below does not apply to data held only on your own device.

Separately from the app, the Scheduler product page on our website offers a “Get started” application form. If you submit it, we receive the name, company, email address, phone number, country, social media handles, audience-size ranges, and comments you enter, and we use them only to review your application and to contact you about the Scheduler. That information is not sold, is not shared with advertising networks or data brokers, and is not used for advertising. The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. To have your application and the information in it deleted, contact us at the address at the end of this Privacy Policy.
